iQuery is an Android application for exploring Apple's App Store.

FEATURES

• Search for iOS applications by name or keyword.
• Browse top free and paid applications.
• Explore top free and paid games.
• Discover new applications and games.
• Browse apps and games by category.
• View application details, descriptions, screenshots, ratings, and prices.
• Read customer reviews and ratings.
• Explore applications from individual developers.
• Switch between 175 supported App Store storefronts.
• Filter search results for iPhone-compatible applications.

DATA SOURCES

iQuery retrieves application information, charts, and customer reviews using Apple's public iTunes APIs.

REQUIREMENTS

Android 8.0 or later.
Internet connection required.
